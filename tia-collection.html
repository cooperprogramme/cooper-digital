Founded in 2007, Tia Collection is a global art collection based in Santa Fe, New Mexico, with a mission to support artists and museum institutions by acquiring and loaning works of art. Tia aims to foster dialogue, stewardship, and scholarship of art through its lending program, partner exhibitions and publications.

Their collection consists of over five thousand works ranging from a variety of mediums, locations, genres and centuries. From emerging artists to world renown names, such as: Georgia O'Keeffe; Damien Hirst; Tracey Emin and Anselm Kiefer. Many works have featured in exhibitions across the globe, however they needed more exposure as pivotal exhibitions missed key works in their collection.

Tia approached us to amplify their services and increase accessibility to their collection online. We produced a new brand direction and digital portal to redefine their core values and reintroduce their offerings to art institutions, helping to engage with their audiences and bring clarity to their message.

Defining their brand identity included the creation of a new typeface 'Tia Sans™', and production of a brand manual to provide guidance on communications and implementation. I travelled to Santa Fe to deliver a three day workshop with their staff, undergoing various iterations to produce digital architecture, wireframe prototypes and art direction, guiding user-interaction with their collection online.

Tia logomark

Grid alignment and spacial relationships.

Visible grid structure

Exposed grid marks communicate the process of storage before loaning works to museums and galleries.

Rotating curated features

Displaying exhibits featuring works currently on loan, revealing the collection's global reach.

Identity

The logomark has a plurality of meanings, the configuration of the circles form two uppercase T's (depicting the initials of the collector's name), which also represents a patriarchal cross (portraying the family-owned collection), the individual circles represent the indian 'bindi' (dot) referencing the owners indian heritage, while the dispersal of gradient circles represents the visual field and depth.

Architecture and userflow

Mapping the journey and navigation system. Ensuring content is purposeful and inter-connected through meaningful relationships.

Tia Sans typeface

Custom drawn modern neo-grotesk font available in four styles: Regular, Italic, Display, Display Italic. The Display styles containing wider letters for impact and weight when used in marketing collateral. Featuring rounded edges to each glyph's beziers, referencing the logomark's circular shape.

Collection online

Presenting a catalogue of the entire collection online, with curated content giving a window into the works.

Searching the catalogue

Including autocomplete, filtering criteria and sort options.

Caption templates

Mounted captions for displays of exhibited works on loan.

Responsiveness

Fully responsive and accessible digital design standards for all devices and browsers.

Artwork features

Ability for audiences to save and curate albums, share publicly, and enquire.

Artists

Alphabetical search interface for artists, contextualising the collection artists and their works.

Empowering audiences

Streamlining collection requests and curation to automate processes for staff administration.
